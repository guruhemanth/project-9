## 5. Summary of Remediations Applied

1. **P0-4 & P1-5 Webhook Events & Dead-Lettering**: Migration 15 created `pay_svc.webhook_events` with unique `event_id`, status tracking (`processing`, `processed`, `failed`, `ignored`), attempt counters, and error logging. Replayed webhooks return immediate `{ idempotent: true }`.
2. **P0-6 Webhook Paid Authority**: Removed client callback bypass and backdoor flags in `/api/payments/verify-signature`. Webhook is now the sole authority to mark a booking as `paid`.
3. **P0-7 Currency Verification**: Webhook enforces `currency === 'INR'` and rejects any mismatch.
4. **P0-8 Balance Workflow**: Created `POST /api/payments/create-balance-order`, tracked `balance_due_date` and `advance_paid_at` on bookings, and finalized gross settlements upon balance payment capture.
5. **P0-9 Commission Snapshotting**: Snapshotted commission rate and exact paise on `POST /api/bookings` directly from the caterer profile.
6. **P0-10 Secure Internal Refund**: Guarded internal refund routes with signed internal JWTs and gateway proxy blocks.
7. **P0-13 Expiration Sweep Test**: Automated integration test for 12-hour expiration background sweep releasing slots.
8. **P0-15 State Machine Matrix**: Exhaustive 49-state Cartesian test suite in `shared/tests/state-machine.test.ts` covering 245 combinations.
9. **P0-16 Double-Booking Concurrency Race**: Atomic availability locking rejects concurrent accept requests on the same date with 409 Conflict.
10. **P0-18 Approved Caterer Verification**: Enforced `caterer.status === 'approved'` on booking creation.
11. **P0-20 Date & Service Area Constraints**: Enforced IST past-date checks, lead-time rules, and city service area validation.
12. **P0-24 Refresh Token Rotation**: Implemented short-lived 15m access tokens, refresh token rotation in `auth_svc.refresh_tokens`, and token family reuse revocation.
13. **P0-26 PII Logging Redaction**: Added phone, address, bank details, FSSAI, GST, PAN, and signatures to Pino log redaction paths.
14. **P0-27 Admin Audit Logging**: Wrote administrative audit logs to `audit_svc.admin_audit_logs` for caterer approvals/rejections and settlement disbursements.
15. **P1-13 Contact Details Masking**: Masked customer contact details (`•`) until the initial deposit is paid.
