7. **P0-11**: Settlement disbursement does not check event completion date or dispute window.
8. **P0-13**: 12-hour booking expiration job lacks an automated test.
9. **P0-15**: Full 49-state Cartesian transition matrix lacks unit test coverage.
10. **P0-16**: Double booking unique constraint lacks a concurrent race test (`Promise.all`).
11. **P0-18**: `POST /api/bookings` does not verify `caterer.status === 'approved'`.
12. **P0-20**: Booking request creation lacks validation for past event dates, `lead_time_days`, and city/service-area matching.

## 5. Fix First: Top 5 Most Dangerous Gaps

Ordered by security and financial impact:

1. **P0-6: Client-side payment callback bypasses webhook to mark booking paid**
   - *Why it matters*: An attacker can send forged signatures or exploit `'test_mode_instant_approval'` to `POST /api/payments/verify-signature` and confirm bookings as paid without Razorpay ever receiving funds.
   - *Smallest change*: Remove `UPDATE order_svc.bookings SET status = 'paid'` from `/api/payments/verify-signature`; treat it solely as an optimistic client acknowledgment and update booking status to `paid` exclusively in the verified webhook handler.
2. **P0-10: `/api/payments/internal/refund` lacks authentication / role check**
   - *Why it matters*: Any authenticated user can trigger automated Razorpay refunds for arbitrary bookings, draining merchant funds.
   - *Smallest change*: Require `x-internal-token` verification or enforce `authUser.role === 'admin'` in `POST /api/payments/internal/refund` before executing refund requests.
3. **P0-18: `POST /api/bookings` does not verify `caterer.status === 'approved'`**
   - *Why it matters*: Customers can bypass search UI to place orders with rejected, suspended, or unverified caterers by sending their UUID directly.
   - *Smallest change*: Add `AND status = 'approved'` to the caterer query in `backend/services/booking-order/src/index.ts:175`, returning `404 Caterer not found or not approved`.
4. **P0-20: Missing past event date & lead time validation in booking creation**
   - *Why it matters*: Users can book dates in the past (e.g. `2020-01-01`) or book events for tomorrow when the caterer requires a 3-day lead time.
   - *Smallest change*: Add a validation check in `POST /api/bookings` asserting `eventDate >= today + lead_time_days`.
5. **P1-13: Customer phone and email exposed to caterers before deposit payment**
   - *Why it matters*: Caterers can harvest customer contact details on unaccepted/unpaid bookings and negotiate cash/off-platform deals, bypassing platform commission entirely.
   - *Smallest change*: In `backend/services/booking-order/src/index.ts:349, 372`, mask or omit `u.phone` and `u.email` unless `b.status IN ('paid', 'completed')`.
